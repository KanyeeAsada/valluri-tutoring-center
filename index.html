Meet Your Teacher

Kaushiki Valluri is a Computer Engineer who earned her Bachelor of Science from Virginia Tech and pursued her Master's degree in Engineering Management from Cornell University.

Kaushiki has more than 12 years of mathematics tutoring experience, including 7 years of online instruction. She has taught more than 350 students ranging from elementary school through college-level mathematics.

Her teaching style focuses on keeping students engaged, explaining difficult concepts in an easy-to-understand way, and helping students become more confident in mathematics.

In addition to her engineering career, Kaushiki works as a substitute teacher because of her passion for education and working with students.
